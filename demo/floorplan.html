Why this floorplan

Sparse models waste
monolithic arrays. We split the problem.

Occupancy 0.888 vs 0.80 monolithic under measured MoE skew. SRAM holds the chunk so weights stream once per 2048 tokens — not once per token. That is the whole trick.

Sub-tile, don’t overbuild.

Four 64×64 tiles behave like one 128×128 when dense, but waste only a quarter-tile on a short expert. Fold-16 trees keep the wires short.
