Floorplan

The one fact the design turns on. Batch-1 decode is purely memory-bound. At SKU B’s 68.3 GB/s the array needs 119 lanes to keep up — it has 16,384. Everything above that exists for prefill, which is the only compute-bound phase and the only latency a user feels. That is why the array is sized for time-to-first-token and power-gated during generation.
